## Before you start

You will prepare a short notice for a fictional workshop. By the end you will have two things: a request another person could understand, and a draft checked against the original facts.

You may use an AI chat you already have access to. Aulafy does not open that chat or send your answers. You can also work on paper with the example in step 3. You do not need a new tool or real personal data.

## 1. Choose a small outcome

“Help me with the workshop” leaves too many decisions open. Make it specific: we want **a notice under 80 words** for people who have already signed up. We do not want the AI to send it.

These are all the facts for this exercise:

| Detail | Fictional value |
| --- | --- |
| Activity | Writing workshop |
| Day and time | Saturday, 10:00 to 11:00 |
| Location | Room 2 |
| What to bring | A notebook |
| Price | Not supplied |

Names, phone numbers, email addresses and attendee lists are unnecessary for drafting this notice.

## 2. Write the request

Copy this request or write it in your own words. A *prompt* is simply the request you give the system, not a special formula.

```text
Task: write a notice for people registered for a workshop.
Facts: writing workshop; Saturday from 10:00 to 11:00;
room 2; participants should bring a notebook.
Audience: people who have already signed up.
Boundaries: do not invent prices, registration dates or contacts.
Do not send or publish anything.
Format: a subject and a message under 80 words.
Check: keep unanswered questions separate from the draft.
```

Each line has a purpose: what to produce, which information to use, who it is for, what not to do and how to present it. You can change tools without changing this structure.

## 3. Check the draft

An acceptable result could look like this. This is a written exercise example, **not output collected from a model during this test**.

> **Subject: Writing workshop reminder**
>
> The workshop is on Saturday, from 10:00 to 11:00, in room 2. Remember to bring a notebook. See you there!

Return to the table and check the day, time, room and materials. Then look for added claims: a price, available places or payment instructions. Pleasant wording does not make up for a wrong date.

Your result need not match the example word for word. It must respect the facts and boundaries.

## 4. Correct a specific mistake

If the draft says “register by Friday”, that deadline is not in the facts. Request a precise correction:

```text
You added a registration deadline I did not provide.
Remove it. Keep the other facts unchanged.
Return only the corrected draft.
```

Check the complete text again: fixing one sentence does not prove that the others stayed correct. If the result keeps adding information, finish the notice yourself using the table.

## Your result

Before finishing, check that you have:

- A request that separates facts from boundaries.
- A draft under 80 words with no added facts.
- A check of the four supplied details.
- No automatically sent message.

**What you have learned:** delegating a draft does not mean delegating its verification or delivery. In the next chapter, you will practise spotting an answer that looks right but is not.
